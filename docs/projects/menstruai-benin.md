# MenstruAI Bénin — Project case study

**Category:** FemTech · Digital health  
**Organization:** Shalom Digital Solutions  
**Status:** Initiative / prototype development. Confirm implemented scope before publication.  
**Institutional context:** UNICEF Femtech Ventures application was prepared; do not claim selection, funding or endorsement unless officially confirmed.

## Challenge

Adolescents and young women need accessible, understandable and trustworthy information about menstrual and reproductive health. Digital information must be designed responsibly, validated by qualified health stakeholders and adapted to local contexts.

## Intended users

Adolescents and young women in Benin. Any age-specific experience, safeguarding measure and consent flow must be reviewed with qualified local stakeholders before launch.

## Intended product direction

- Educational content on menstrual health.
- Information about the menstrual cycle.
- Access to reviewed reproductive health information.
- AI-assisted information support as a proposed feature, with clear limits and escalation to qualified professionals.
- Mobile-first user experience.
- Privacy-by-design and data minimization.
- Content review and update process involving qualified health professionals.

## Proposed technical architecture

- Frontend: React.
- Backend: Laravel 13 planned.
- API: REST.
- Database: MySQL / MariaDB.
- AI: assistance module under consideration; disclose its actual status accurately.
- CI/CD: GitHub Actions.

The stack above includes planned choices. Confirm the implementation before describing any component as deployed.

## Safety, health and data governance

- Do not present the tool as a diagnostic service.
- Do not generate personalized medical diagnoses or treatment instructions.
- Health content should be reviewed and approved by qualified professionals.
- Provide clear emergency and referral guidance appropriate to the local context.
- Minimize personal data collection and avoid collecting sensitive data unless strictly necessary and properly governed.
- Define access controls, retention, deletion, incident response and consent processes.
- Conduct security and privacy reviews before real-user testing.
